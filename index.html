Privacy Policy

Hole in Calm (com.namib.holeincalm)
Publisher: Thanapat Wongchalermthan
Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy describes how Thanapat Wongchalermthan ("we") handles information in the Android game Hole in Calm. It applies only to this app. It is not legal advice.

Hole in Calm is a single-player tilt game. You do not create an account. We do not run our own servers for gameplay, and we do not add our own analytics or crash-reporting tools. Information is either kept on your device, or collected by Google when a rewarded ad is shown.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves, and we do not receive the information the advertising SDK collects. The game has no accounts.

When a rewarded ad is shown, Google Mobile Ads may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, including the Google Advertising ID, to show ads, measure them, and limit fraud.
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address. The app does not ask for GPS or precise location permission.
  • App activity and diagnostics, including how you interact with an ad, device and app details (such as model, Android version, and this app’s name and version), and crash or performance data about the ads SDK.

Tilt control uses the phone’s motion sensors on the device only. Those readings are not uploaded by us.

Saved on your device

So the game can resume, it stores the following in its private app storage:

  • Stage progress, stars, and best times.
  • Options: tilt sensitivity, inverted controls, music, sound effects, vibration, and calibration.
  • Which balls you have unlocked.
  • Your Endless best time and how many Endless runs you have left today.

This stays on your device. Other apps cannot read it. Choosing Share score builds a score image on the device and opens Android’s share sheet. We do not receive that image. It goes only to the app you choose.

How we use information

  • Run the game and remember progress on your device.
  • Show optional rewarded ads and measure ad performance.
  • Find ad-related crashes and prevent fraud or abuse in ads.
  • Respond to requests you send to pat23.w@gmail.com.

Rewarded ads are optional. You can finish all 29 stages with the Classic ball without watching an ad. An ad can unlock an extra ball, or refill Endless runs after the free daily runs are used. The reward is given only if the ad reports that it was earned.

Third-party services

The release build includes these Google services and no others:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads: rewarded ads, ad measurement, and fraud prevention. Google’s privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Google User Messaging Platform: shows a consent form where required (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland) and stores your ad-privacy choice.

The app does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Play Games, or a billing SDK. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages data collection.

Advertising and your choices

Hole in Calm shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Ad partners may use device identifiers and approximate location to serve and measure ads. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized.

Where Google requires it, the game asks for consent before loading ads. You can change that choice in the game under Options → Ad privacy choices.

On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path varies by device). You can also manage Google’s ad information at https://myadcenter.google.com.

Permissions

The game itself asks only for vibration, used for optional haptic feedback. Loading ads and consent messages needs internet access. The Google Mobile Ads library also declares the Advertising ID permission, ad-services permissions, network state, and a few technical permissions its libraries use (including a wake lock and a foreground-service permission from its background-work library). The game does not start its own foreground service, and it does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files.

Children

Hole in Calm is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no chat and no in-app purchases. If you believe a child has provided personal information, email us and we will help.

Data retention and deletion

Progress and settings remain on your device until you erase them. To delete them, open Android Settings → Apps → Hole in Calm → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

We do not hold accounts or a copy of your gameplay. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own policies. To ask about information we control, email pat23.w@gmail.com with the app name. We may ask only for what we need to understand the request.

Security

Ad and consent traffic uses HTTPS. No method of transmission is completely secure. Local save data is kept in the app’s private storage.

Changes

We may update this page if the game, ads, or the law changes. The "Last updated" date will change when we do. The Play listing will point at this same address.
